How a key visual gets made

The usual art direction process, with generative AI as one of the production tools. Every image is directed and finished by hand.

  1. 1

    Concept

    Idea, headline and a rough layout agreed with copy and client before production starts.

  2. 2

    Blocking

    Composition, camera and light set up with 3D blocking, photo references or a rough composite.

  3. 3

    Controlled generation

    ComfyUI workflows on Stable Diffusion, with pose and depth control to keep characters and products consistent.

  4. 4

    Finishing

    Upscaling in Magnific, then Photoshop compositing, retouching, color grading and type.

  5. 5

    Rollout

    A layout system that carries the idea to every format: billboard, stadium screen, feed, Story, landing page.
